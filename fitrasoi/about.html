Why this exists

Built for hostel kitchens, not studio kitchens.

Most "high protein" recipe content online assumes an air fryer, a stocked pantry, and a food processor. FitRasoi assumes a 2x3 room, one shared kettle, and a kirana store within walking distance.

The rules every recipe follows

Five constraints, no exceptions

Every recipe on this site was written against the same checklist before it earned a place here.

  • 01Under 20 minutes, most under 10 — between classes, before the mess shuts, or right after a workout.
  • 02No oven, no gas stove, no food processor — just an induction plate, a pan, and sometimes a kettle.
  • 03Under ₹35 per serving — priced against an actual kirana store, not a metro supermarket.
  • 04Ingredients you can name — no "protein isolate" or specialty powders unless they're genuinely optional.
  • 05At least 10g of protein per serving — small enough to actually cook, substantial enough to matter.
The person behind it

A portfolio project, cooked with real hostel logic

FitRasoi is a frontend project built to show semantic HTML, hand-written CSS design systems, and vanilla JS interactivity — no frameworks, no build step. Every recipe, price and cook time was worked out the way a hostel resident actually would: by checking what's realistically in a PG room.
