The hostel mess problem

Mess food fills your plate, not your protein target.

Most hostel and PG meals are rice, roti and dal-heavy but protein-light. If you're training, that gap shows up as slow recovery and stalled progress — not a supplements problem, a plate problem.

Recovery slows down

Muscles repair using protein. Without enough of it daily, soreness lingers longer and strength gains stall even if your workouts are solid.

Supplements aren't the fix

A ₹2000 whey tub can't undo a low-protein plate. Whole foods you already trust — eggs, paneer, dal, soya — get you most of the way for a fraction of the cost.

Small, repeatable swaps work

You don't need to cook elaborate meals. One high-protein addition per meal — sprouts at breakfast, chana at snack time — closes most of the gap.

Breakfast · 10 min

Besan Cheela

🥣 ~15g protein ₹15 Induction + pan

A savory gram-flour pancake — whisk, pour, flip. Crisp outside, soft inside, no fermentation or waiting needed.

Ingredients (serves 1, makes 2 cheela)

  • ½ cup besan (gram flour)
  • ¼ cup water, added slowly
  • 1 small onion + 1 green chilli, finely chopped
  • Pinch of turmeric, salt, ajwain (optional)
  • 1 tsp oil

Steps

  1. Whisk besan with water into a smooth, flowing batter — no lumps.
  2. Mix in onion, chilli, turmeric and salt.
  3. Heat a greased pan, pour a ladle of batter, spread thin.
  4. Cook 2 minutes each side on medium heat until golden spots appear.
  5. Serve with curd or ketchup.
Snack · No cook · 5 min

Sprouts Chaat

🥣 ~11g protein ₹10 No cooking

Soak sprouts overnight, and this comes together in the time it takes to chop an onion. Tangy, crunchy, zero heat source needed.

Ingredients (serves 1)

  • 1 cup moong sprouts (pre-soaked/store-bought)
  • 2 tbsp roasted peanuts
  • 1 small tomato + onion, chopped
  • Lemon juice, chaat masala, salt

Steps

  1. Rinse sprouts well. Optionally steam 2 minutes to soften.
  2. Toss with chopped tomato, onion, and peanuts.
  3. Add lemon juice, chaat masala and salt. Mix and eat fresh.
Meal · 20 min

Soya Chunks Curry

🥣 ~26g protein ₹18 Induction + pan

The highest-protein recipe on this list. Soya chunks soak up whatever masala you cook them in — this is the simple onion-tomato version.

Ingredients (serves 1)

  • 50g dry soya chunks
  • 1 onion + 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste (optional)
  • ½ tsp each turmeric, chilli powder, garam masala
  • 1 tsp oil, salt

Steps

  1. Boil soya chunks in water 5 minutes, drain and squeeze out water.
  2. Heat oil, sauté onion till golden, add ginger-garlic paste.
  3. Add tomato and spices, cook till soft and oil separates.
  4. Add soya chunks and a splash of water, simmer 5 minutes.
  5. Serve with roti or rice.
