Besan Cheela

🥣 ~15g protein₹15Induction + pan

Savory gram-flour pancake — whisk, pour, flip. No fermenting or waiting.

Ingredients (serves 1, makes 2)

  • ½ cup besan (gram flour)
  • ¼ cup water, added slowly
  • 1 small onion + 1 green chilli, chopped
  • Turmeric, salt, ajwain (optional)
  • 1 tsp oil

Steps

  1. Whisk besan with water into a smooth, lump-free batter.
  2. Mix in onion, chilli, turmeric and salt.
  3. Pour a ladle onto a greased hot pan, spread thin.
  4. Cook 2 minutes each side until golden spots appear.
  5. Serve with curd or ketchup.
Breakfast · 8 min

Anda Bhurji

🥣 ~16g protein₹20Induction + pan

Spiced scrambled eggs — the fastest hostel-room protein hit there is.

Ingredients (serves 1)

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 green chilli
  • Turmeric, chilli powder, salt
  • 1 tsp oil or butter

Steps

  1. Heat oil, sauté onion and chilli till soft.
  2. Add tomato and spices, cook till mushy.
  3. Crack eggs directly into the pan.
  4. Scramble and mix for 2-3 minutes.
  5. Serve with bread or roti.
Snack · No cook · 5 min

Sprouts Chaat

🥣 ~11g protein₹10No cooking

Tangy, crunchy, and needs zero heat source — just a bowl and a spoon.

Ingredients (serves 1)

  • 1 cup moong sprouts (pre-soaked/store-bought)
  • 2 tbsp roasted peanuts
  • 1 tomato + onion, chopped
  • Lemon juice, chaat masala, salt

Steps

  1. Rinse sprouts well; steam 2 minutes if you like them softer.
  2. Toss with tomato, onion and peanuts.
  3. Add lemon juice, chaat masala and salt, mix and eat fresh.

Peanut-Jaggery Bites

🥣 ~3g / bite₹12 / batchInduction (5 min)

Batch-make once, snack all week. Makes 6 — grab two before the gym.

Ingredients (makes 6 bites)

  • ½ cup roasted peanuts, crushed
  • 2 tbsp jaggery, grated
  • 1 tsp ghee
  • Pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Melt jaggery with ghee on low flame until it forms a soft ball when dropped in water.
  2. Remove from heat, mix in crushed peanuts and salt.
  3. Roll into small balls while still warm.
  4. Cool fully before storing in a dry box.

Chana-Curd Bowl

🥣 ~19g protein₹15No cooking

The laziest high-protein snack on this list — genuinely 2 minutes.

Ingredients (serves 1)

  • ½ cup roasted chana (dalia)
  • 1 cup curd
  • Chaat masala
  • Roasted cumin powder
  • Coriander leaves

Steps

  1. Mix roasted chana into curd.
  2. Sprinkle chaat masala and cumin powder.
  3. Top with coriander. Eat immediately for crunch, or after 5 minutes for a softer bite.

Paneer Bhurji

🥣 ~18g protein₹35Induction + pan

Crumbled paneer in a quick onion-tomato masala. Feels indulgent, isn't.

Ingredients (serves 1)

  • 100g paneer, crumbled
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 green chilli
  • Turmeric, chilli powder, garam masala
  • 1 tsp oil, coriander leaves

Steps

  1. Heat oil, sauté onion and chilli till translucent.
  2. Add tomato and spices, cook till soft.
  3. Add crumbled paneer, mix gently 2-3 minutes — don't overcook.
  4. Garnish with coriander, serve with roti.

Soya Chunks Curry

🥣 ~26g protein₹18Induction + pan

The highest-protein recipe here. Soya chunks soak up whatever masala you cook them in.

Ingredients (serves 1)

  • 50g dry soya chunks
  • 1 onion + 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste (optional)
  • Turmeric, chilli powder, garam masala
  • 1 tsp oil, salt

Steps

  1. Boil soya chunks 5 minutes, drain and squeeze out water.
  2. Sauté onion till golden, add ginger-garlic paste.
  3. Add tomato and spices, cook till oil separates.
  4. Add soya chunks with a splash of water, simmer 5 minutes.
  5. Serve with roti or rice.

Rajma Chawal Shortcut

🥣 ~15g protein₹25Cooker + pan

Uses pre-boiled or canned rajma to skip the overnight soak. Sunday comfort food, any day.

Ingredients (serves 1)

  • 1 cup boiled or canned rajma
  • 1 onion + 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • Rajma masala or garam masala
  • 1 cup cooked rice

Steps

  1. Sauté onion till golden, add ginger-garlic paste.
  2. Add tomato and spices, cook till oil separates.
  3. Add rajma with a little water, simmer 8-10 minutes, mashing a few beans to thicken.
  4. Serve hot over rice.
Drink · No cook · 3 min

PB-Banana Shake

🥣 ~12g protein₹25Blender / jar

Post-gym in a glass. Add a scoop of whey if you have it, for +20g more.

Ingredients (serves 1)

  • 1 cup milk (or plant milk)
  • 1 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 banana
  • Ice cubes (optional)
  • 1 tsp honey (optional)

Steps

  1. Add all ingredients to a blender, or a jar with a tight lid.
  2. Blend until smooth, or shake vigorously for 1-2 minutes.
  3. Pour and drink immediately.
